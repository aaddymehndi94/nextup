# nexttupp web app (Home, Title, Watch)

Scope: every screen of the browser client. Mode: Operate (browse, pick, play), with the title hero and
billboard leaning Experience: the artwork leads, the interface recedes.

Audience and scene: the owner, evenings, low light; laptop, phone (390px) and a TV browser at 1920px driven
by keyboard or remote. Job: search or browse, open a title, press Play on the best source, or choose another
from the full list. Proof: real Cinemeta artwork, live seeds and peers. Constraints: no invented features or
data; keep e2e selectors; recommendation rows, My List, like/dislike and profiles arrive later, so actions
and rows must have room for them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category canon played straight, at the craft of Apple TV, Netflix, Mubi and A24: artwork is the
interface, chrome is black and white. Refuses the old warm-cream italic-serif look and any neon-accent dark UI.

OWN-WORLD: Projection-room black (#0a0a0b) with lifted neutral greys; white is the only action colour;
green/amber/red appear only as swarm health, plus amber for honest playback warnings. One superfamily, Mona Sans: Expanded 125/800 for the
wordmark and title-card headings, standard width for UI, tabular figures only on running clocks. Pill actions, 10px-radius
artwork with a hairline edge, 8% white rules.

STORY: The visitor sees what is popular, searches anything, lands on a page that presents the film like the
service owns it, presses Play, and only opens the source table when it matters.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: full-bleed billboard of the most popular film (backdrop, logo art, meta, synopsis, one
white action), a bar with wordmark and search over it, the first row rising into its lower edge. Title:
backdrop to ~85vh, logo art, meta, synopsis, white Play with room for two round icon buttons, best-source line.

FORM: canon, brief-pinned (Apple TV, Netflix, Mubi, A24); seed: none (brief-pinned, no roll). Signature move:
the wordmark's shared "tt" crossbar and expanded title-card headings.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

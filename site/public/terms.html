Terms of use.

The short version: nexttupp recommends films, series and music and plays what you pick on your own computer. It doesn’t host or sell anything. What you choose to play is your responsibility, so play only what you have the right to.

Last updated 15 November 2026.

What nexttupp is

nexttupp is a free app for your computer. It shows information about films, series and music from public catalogues, with posters, ratings and trailers, and learns what you like to recommend more. Using the app, this website or an account means you agree to these terms.

What nexttupp doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t host, store, upload or sell any film, episode or song. There are no nexttupp servers holding media.
  • It doesn’t check who owns what you play, or whether you’re allowed to.
  • Your account holds no films, episodes or files, and nothing about where they came from: only your email, profiles and taste (see What we keep).

Sources, and what you’re responsible for

When you press Play, the app looks for the title on public file-sharing networks (BitTorrent) and plays it on your computer. BitTorrent itself is legal and widely used, but much of what people share on it is copyrighted, and the law on watching or downloading it differs from country to country.

  • You choose what to play, and you’re responsible for it. Play only what you own, what is in the public domain, or what you otherwise have the right to watch where you live.
  • While something plays, and while you keep a download, your computer also shares pieces of it with other people on the network, as BitTorrent always does. They can see your computer’s internet (IP) address.
  • Music comes from YouTube, played on your computer. YouTube’s own terms apply to it.

If you own a work

nexttupp hosts nothing, so there is nothing on our servers to take down. If you hold the rights to a work and want nexttupp to stop finding or recommending it, open an issue on GitHub naming the work, and we’ll leave it out of future versions.

Your account

  • Accounts are free and open to anyone. You sign in with your Google account: Google tells us your email address and first name, and Google knows you use nexttupp. An account made earlier with the same email is the same account.
  • Don’t use nexttupp, the website or its servers to harm anyone, overload them, or get into anything that isn’t yours. We can disable accounts that do.
  • To delete your account and everything it holds, ask us through the same GitHub link (leave your email address out of the issue; we’ll reply there).

What we keep

Only what syncing needs: your email address and first name (from Google); your profile names; the films and series you like, dislike, list or have played and how far, by their IMDb number; the songs and artists you like or play and the mixes you ask for, with the songs’ names, album and cover so your other computers can show them; and your computers’ names and home-network addresses (so your phone can find them). Never the files, what you searched for, or where anything came from. We don’t sell any of it or show ads. Everything else stays on your computer.

No guarantees

nexttupp is a free project, given as it is. It can have mistakes, stop working, or change, and we can’t promise any title will be found or will play. As far as the law allows, we aren’t liable for any loss from using it, or for what you choose to play.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top says when they last changed; if a change matters, the app will tell you.

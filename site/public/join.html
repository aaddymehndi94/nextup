Join nexttupp.

It’s free and open to everyone. Films and series play in the nexttupp app on your computer, not on this website. Here’s how to get going.

  1. Download and install

    nexttupp is free for Windows, Mac and Linux.

    Download nexttupp

    All downloads

  2. Make a free account

    It takes a few seconds. It keeps your likes, lists and progress the same on every computer, and lets you watch on your phone. You can also skip it.

    Create a free account

    Then sign in to the app with it: click your profile picture in the top-right corner, then:

    Account Sign in

    Or make the account in the app itself, under Account › Create account.

  3. Watch on your phone, too

    Keep nexttupp open on your computer. On your phone, go to nexttupp.com/watch and sign in with the same account. Films play on your phone, streamed from your computer over your home Wi‑Fi.

Trouble opening the app the first time? See the questions for the one-time steps on Mac and Windows.

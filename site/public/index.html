Questions

Can I watch on this website?

No. Films and series play in the nexttupp app, on your computer. This website is where you download the app and make your account. Once the app is open on your computer, you can watch on your phone too, at nexttupp.com/watch.

Is it free?

Yes. There’s nothing to buy and no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. nexttupp works fully without one. An account keeps your likes, lists, profiles and progress the same on every computer, and lets you watch on your phone. Anyone can make one for free, right here or in the app: click your profile picture, then Account, then Create account.

My Mac says nexttupp can’t be opened.

The app isn’t signed with Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it the first time. In Applications, right-click nexttupp and choose Open, then choose Open again.

On macOS Sequoia and later, try to open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. After that it opens normally.

Windows says it protected my PC.

That’s SmartScreen, which flags apps that aren’t signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Which Linux file should I pick?

The AppImage runs on most distributions: allow it to run as a program (in its file properties, or with chmod +x), then open it. On Ubuntu, Debian and their relatives, the .deb installs it like any other app.

Can I watch on my phone or TV?

Yes, with your computer doing the work. Keep nexttupp open and signed in on your computer. On your phone, go to nexttupp.com/watch, sign in with the same account and tap Connect: films then play on your phone over your Wi‑Fi. For a TV with Chromecast or Google TV built in, press Cast in the player. There’s no separate phone or TV app yet.

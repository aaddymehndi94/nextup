nexttupp on your TV.

For Android TV and Google TV. Films play on the TV from nexttupp on your computer, so the TV needs to be on the same Wi‑Fi as the computer, with nexttupp open on it. Setting it up takes a few minutes, once. No nexttupp on your computer yet? Start here.

  1. Get the Downloader app on the TV

    On the TV, open the Google Play Store, search for Downloader (the orange one, by AFTVnews) and install it. It’s free.

  2. Type nexttupp’s address

    Open Downloader, select its address box, type this and press Go:

    nexttupp.com/tv.apk

    That’s two t’s and two p’s, all in small letters.

  3. Allow it, the first time

    The TV asks whether Downloader may install apps. Choose Settings, turn Downloader on, press Back, then choose Install.

    No switch for Downloader? Go to Settings, then System, then About, select Android TV OS build seven times, and try again.

  4. Connect it to your computer

    When it’s installed, choose Open (later, nexttupp is with your TV’s other apps). It finds your computer on the same Wi‑Fi and asks for a code. On the computer, open nexttupp, go to Account, and under On your TV choose Add a TV. Type the four digits on the TV, and you’re watching.

To update the TV app later, open Downloader and go to the same address. You won’t need a new code. Installing another way, like from a USB stick? Download the app file.
